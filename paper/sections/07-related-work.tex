\parhead{Block formats beyond the matrix engine.}%
Prior work has shown that block formats can cross the matrix/non-matrix operator boundary, in operator-specific engines.
DB-Attn shares exponents between block floating-point matrix multiplication and softmax, with a softmax engine in RTL \cite{Wang2025DBAttn}.
MXInt ViT maps every operator of a vision transformer into the MXInt domain \citeTBD{MXInt ViT}, and MASE generates dataflow accelerators whose inter-operator tensors stay in MXInt \citeTBD{MASE}.
BBAL adds a nonlinear unit in its BBFP format, but only after converting its inputs back from FP16 \cite{Han2025BBAL}.
We ask instead when keeping the block representation pays off between separate, reused units, comparing placements as configurations of one RTL.

\parhead{Data and metadata consistency, tags and write combining.}%
CHERI binds a tag atomically to its data and clears it on an ordinary write, per capability \citeTBD{CHERI ISA specification}; it does not cover multi-row numerical objects read again under a lease after a statistic is computed.
Tag comparison in tagged-token dataflow and Triggered Instructions is a precedent for checking at the consumer \citeTBD{tagged-token dataflow; Triggered Instructions}.
Write combining is common practice and our strong baseline for hypothesis~B (\secref{sec:metadata}), not a contribution.
The commit, lease and publish rules (\secref{sec:retain}, \secref{sec:commit}) belong to our engineering mechanism: we report its cost and do not claim that correct metadata handling is itself new.
